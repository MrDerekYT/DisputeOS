From our family to yours

It's a mom, her daughters and a lot of lemons

We're a family stand from Miami-Dade. You'll find us under a white tent, behind a hot pink tablecloth, pressing lemons by hand and pouring them over ice.

Sofia's Fresh Lemonade is a family-owned business based in Miami, Florida, founded and operated by a dedicated mother and her daughters. Our family takes pride in serving freshly squeezed lemonade made with quality ingredients, love, and a passion for bringing refreshing flavors to every event.
